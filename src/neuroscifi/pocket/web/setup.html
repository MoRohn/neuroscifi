Set up Pocket on this phone

About two minutes, once per phone. Your phone learns to trust your Spark, so everything between them stays private.

This setup page is resting

For safety it only offers the certificate for 15 minutes after you choose Add a phone. On your Spark, open the console and choose Add a phone again (or run nsf add-phone), then scan the first code once more.

  1. Download the certificate

    It is a small file that tells this phone your Spark is genuine. It is not an app and holds no secrets.

    Download certificate

    Tap Allow when your iPhone asks, then Close.

    If your phone asks what the file is for, choose VPN and apps and name it NeuroSciFi.

  2. Install it

    Open Settings. Tap Profile Downloaded near the top, then Install in the corner. Enter your passcode and tap Install once more.

  3. Switch on trust

    Still in Settings, go to General › About › Certificate Trust Settings and switch on NeuroSciFi. Then come back to this page.

  4. Install it

    Open Settings and search for CA certificate. Choose Install a certificate › CA certificate › Install anyway, and pick neuroscifi-ca.crt from your downloads. Then come back to this page.

    Menu names differ a little between phone makers. On Samsung: Settings › Security and privacy › More security settings › Install from device storage › CA certificate.

  5. Install it

    Open the downloaded file and add it to this device's trusted certificates (for websites). Then come back to this page.

  6. Pair with your Spark

    Checking whether this phone trusts your Spark…

    Now scan the second code on your Spark's screen with your camera, and tap Pair.

Check it is really your Spark

Your Spark's screen shows a fingerprint next to the codes. After installing, open the certificate's details on this phone and compare its SHA-256 fingerprint with that one. If they differ, remove the certificate and ask the owner of your Spark.

iPhone: Settings › General › VPN & Device Management › NeuroSciFi › More Details.

Android: Settings › search for Trusted credentials › User › NeuroSciFi.

This page cannot show the fingerprint itself: anything on it could come from an impostor on your network. Do this on a home network you trust.
